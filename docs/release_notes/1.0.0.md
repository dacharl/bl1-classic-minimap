# Classic Minimap 1.0.0

The first stable release: an Enhanced-style minimap for **Borderlands Game of the Year (classic)**, as a single PythonSDK mod.

It sits in the top-right corner of the HUD, tilted like the rest of the HUD, and shows:

- the current zone's map art, scrolling and centered on you (optionally rotating with you);
- landmarks: New-U and fast-travel stations, vendors, Catch-A-Ride, bounty boards and area exits;
- your current objective as a diamond, or the turn-in mark when the mission is ready to hand in;
- a "!" where a new mission can be picked up and a turn-in mark where a finished one can be handed in;
- a north marker and edge arrows for objectives that are off the map window;
- enemies as red dots, taken from the same radar the compass uses.

While the map is shown, the ECHO panel (including the Angel videos), the mission title and the objective checklist move left to make room, as they do in Enhanced. The minimap hides itself during the travel tunnel, in menus, in cutscenes and when you're down.

## What's in this release

- **One-file install.** The mod builds its HUD movie on your machine, from your own `GfxHUD.upk` and `menus_ingame_redux.upk` (and Enhanced's `GfxHUD.upk` when Enhanced is installed), and caches it. The download contains no game files and your game files are never modified.
- **Enhanced art when you have it, original art when you don't.** With Borderlands GOTY Enhanced installed, the minimap uses Enhanced's box, marks and enemy diamond. Without it, the mod draws its own: the "N", a gold "!" and a green "?" in the game's HUD font, a dark frame, and a red enemy diamond.
- **Options under MODS → Classic Minimap**, grouped into Map, Markers, HUD, Custom position and Advanced: docked size, zoom and zoom mode, icon size, rotate with player, enemies and their range, mission givers, objective tint, compass, Angel position, artwork choice, and a button to rebuild the HUD movie.
- **Docked or custom position.** Docked is where Enhanced puts it. Custom has its own size and margins, with no tilt and no HUD changes.
- **Angel's ECHO video** can be centered at the top, clear of the minimap, as in Enhanced.
- **A matching save icon.** While the map is docked, the game's autosave icon is replaced by a Vault logo beside the map, so it doesn't cover the minimap.
- **Extended UI works.** Both mods replace the HUD movie, so the minimap merges itself into Extended UI's movie at startup (a pause of a few seconds, once per session).
- **Keys:** F8 refreshes the map markers and refits the zoom. F7 writes a diagnostic snapshot to the SDK log; please include it with bug reports.

## Known limitations

- Single player: in co-op, other players aren't shown as markers, and split-screen layouts fall back to the Custom position.
- Classic has no player-placed map waypoint, so Enhanced's waypoint marker has no equivalent.
- Played on the PC (with and without Enhanced's art, and with Extended UI) and on the Steam Deck. The 4:3 layout hasn't been played yet, so the save icon and Angel's video there are unchecked.

## Upgrading

Options saved by 0.15 or earlier go back to their defaults once. If you used v0.12 or earlier, delete `GfxHUD_Minimap.upk` from `WillowGame/CookedPC/Packages/Interface`; it is no longer used.

Bug reports are welcome: open an issue with the F7 snapshot (or `Binaries/Plugins/unrealsdk.log`) and a screenshot.
